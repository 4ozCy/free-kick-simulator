RETRO FREE KICK

Bend it, dip it, knuckle it. A pocket-sized 16-bit free-kick game with real ball physics, a keeper who learns your habits, and far too much chaos.

HOW TO PLAY

  • Swipe up from the ball to shoot. Direction is your aim, length is your power, and a curved swipe adds whip.
  • Or set AIM and PWR on the bars and hit KICK!
  • Drag the crosshair on the ball widget to pick where your boot strikes it. Off-centre curls it, low lifts it over the wall, high makes it dip.
  • Pick a kick: POWER, CURVE, TRIVELA (outside of the boot, late swerve) or KNUCKLE (no spin, wobbles and dies late).

WHAT'S INSIDE

  • 18 original legends, each with their own preferred foot, stance, run-up, signature kick and perk.
  • A smart keeper who reads the angle, takes a second look at curling shots, and learns which side you keep scoring on. Mix it up.
  • 33 CHAOS events: gusts, a dog that heads your shot, a cat that scares the keeper the wrong way, everyone tripping over their shoelaces, the wall going on strike...
  • Streak multipliers, 3-star levels, a skill ladder from Beginner to Legend with perks, and unlockable balls.
  • Replays you can save and rewatch, plus a SLOW-MO mode for the whole shot.
  • Silly toggles: 1000 km/h shots, mega curve, and moving the ball anywhere you like.

GOOD TO KNOW

  • Plays best in portrait. On a phone, tap fullscreen.
  • Your progress saves in your browser. No ads, no accounts, no tracking.
  • All art and sound are generated in code. All players and kits are fictional.
