# Retro Free Kick

A free 16-bit style pixel free-kick game for Android and the browser. Bend it, dip it, knuckle it.

**Play and download: [retrofreekick.site](https://retrofreekick.site/)**

- Play in the browser: [retrofreekick.site/play](https://retrofreekick.site/play/)
- Android APK: [latest release](https://github.com/4ozCy/retro-free-kick/releases/latest)
- Soundtrack: [retrofreekick.site/music](https://retrofreekick.site/music/)

18 players, real-ish ball physics, a keeper who learns, 37 chaos events, a daily challenge and an original samba and jersey club soundtrack made in code.

Made by one human (the ideas) and an AI (the code). MIT licensed.
